--------------------------------------------------
51. IMPORTANT TRUST & SAFETY UX
--------------------------------------------------

Throughout the application, clearly distinguish:

EXTRACTED
Information read from uploaded documents.

REFERENCE
Information obtained from demo/reference databases or GIS.

SYSTEM FINDING
A result generated by deterministic validation/anomaly rules.

AI EXPLANATION
An explanation generated by the AI assistant.

HUMAN VERIFIED
A decision explicitly confirmed by an authorized demo official.

Never merge these into one category called "Truth."

Use labels such as:

"Document says..."
"Reference data shows..."
"System detected..."
"AI explanation..."
"Human verification..."

This distinction is a major part of the product's credibility.
